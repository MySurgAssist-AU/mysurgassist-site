[ What We Do ]

The whole journey of surgical assisting, in one place.

Surgical assistants are vital to the world of surgery. From technical skill in the operation to exceptional patient outcomes, they are integral to the economy of surgery as a whole.

MySurgAssist links enthusiastic, talented surgical assistants with established consultant surgeons, through a personable, experience driven service that brings both parties together efficiently. We are run by experienced surgical assistants, with second-to-none knowledge of what assistants and surgeons each need. The aim is a streamlined, hassle free experience that brings both groups together seamlessly.

[ Our Reputation ]

Trusted by surgeons because we are assistants.

We are surgical assistants, and we built MySurgAssist as the ideal service for everything an assistant needs to work and everything a surgeon needs to run a list. As a result, MySurgAssist has built a formidable reputation amongst surgeons. A trusted, reliable brand, we make sure both sides benefit from our recruitment process and our expertise in surgical assisting.

[ Our Services ]

Recruitment, billing and information, run by people who do the job. We find the lists and fill them. We bill every case and pay fortnightly, whether or not the list came through us. And we advise on all of it, from hospital accreditation to what to expect from each surgeon.

[ Billing ]
All your billing,
handled.

Every case you assist, billed by us and paid to you fortnightly, whether or not the list came through MSA. Health funds and Medicare, patient gap payments, precollected fees and top ups, WorkCover and CTP. You assist. We do the rest.
