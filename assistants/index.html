MySurgAssist liaises between surgeon and assistant so the expected pay is clear before the list. We advocate for competitive remuneration, but the business model of each practice is theirs.

[ Who Can Assist ]

Doctors, nurses and PNSAs.

[ Medical Practitioners ]
Doctors are the default: residents, registrars, fellows, GPs and specialists. Assisting is billed to Medicare and the health funds under your own provider number.
[ Nurse Practitioners and PNSAs ]
We also take nurse practitioners and perioperative nurse surgical assistants. Because a nurse assistant cannot be billed to Medicare in the traditional way, MySurgAssist runs an opt in programme for surgeons who want a nurse assistant and pay for one directly. It is a harder field to recruit for and we do our best.

[ Credentialling ]

Credentialling is frustrating. We make it easier.

Hospital credentialling is frustrating. It is not centralised, so you credential yourself hospital by hospital, even across several hospitals in the same group. It is your responsibility, and we make it as easy as we can: our hospital guide has the contact details and a walkthrough for each hospital, and we help where we can.

Often you need a list before a hospital will credential you, and a list before you can be credentialled, which is the chicken and egg of assisting. You can apply for a list at a hospital where you are not yet credentialled. Be realistic about the time it takes, and apply far enough ahead to push the credentialling through before the day.

A variety of skills will be required, and expectations vary by specialty and surgeon. Aim to be familiar with suturing, urinary catheterisation and writing operation reports. Many surgeons will teach you on the job, and it is our job to match skill level to the appropriate procedure.

[ Questions ]

The things assistants ask us.

How much does it cost?

5% plus GST of your fee, on every list we bill. More on the Billing page.

What if I can no longer attend?

The more notice the better, so MySurgAssist can find a suitable replacement. Cancelling last minute without an appropriate reason may affect your ability to find lists in the future.

What if my list is cancelled?

It happens. Surgeons cancel lists, sometimes at short notice and sometimes without explanation, and an assistant can turn out not to be required. Like a rideshare app, we connect the two sides and we cannot control a cancellation on either. There is a necessary volatility to this work. We ask practices to be mindful of it, and we do our best to find you something else.

Do I have to keep working through MSA?

Ongoing work with a surgeon MSA introduced you to continues through MSA. It is in the app Terms and Conditions, and it is what keeps the network working for everyone in it.

Is work guaranteed?

No. Lists depend on surgeons, hospitals and the time of year, and we cannot guarantee work at any point, regardless of tier. What we can do is put every list in front of you and back you when you apply.
