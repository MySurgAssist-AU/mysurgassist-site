[ Questions ]

The things surgeons ask us.

What does it cost the practice?

Nothing. Recruitment is free for surgeons and practices. MSA’s fee comes out of the assistant’s fee, never added to yours.

Is an assistant guaranteed?

No. We cannot force an assistant to take a list. There is no set time to fill one: some go in seconds, some sit for a month. Organisation is rewarded. A clear list with a financial guide, posted early, is the one that fills.

What if I need someone today?

Post it. Assistants are often available at short notice in the event of emergency, and we chase it directly, but nothing is guaranteed.

Can I keep the same assistant?

Yes. Name them as preferred when you post, and ongoing work with an assistant MSA introduced continues through MSA.
